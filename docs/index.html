🧭 the nine questions, in order

Ask yourself these, before you risk a cent

Each answer narrows the next — your goal shapes your budget, your risk profile shapes your timing, and so on, all the way down to which broker actually fits. Start wherever you are.

  1. 01

    Goal

    What's this money actually for — and roughly when do you need it?

  2. 02

    Budget

    How much can safely go in, once your emergency fund and debts are covered?

  3. 03

    Risk profile

    How much loss could you handle — financially, and honestly, emotionally?

  4. 04

    Timing

    All at once, or spread out over time? And how does that shift as your goal date nears?

  5. 05

    Market

    Which geography or sector are you actually researching — home, global, or both?

  6. 06

    Asset

    Stocks, funds, bonds, property — which vehicle actually fits the plan so far?

  7. 07

    Diversification

    How is the risk spread, so no single company or market can sink the whole plan?

  8. 08

    Strategy

    What are the written rules that keep you steady when the market gets loud?

  9. 09

    Broker

    Where do you actually execute — and, once it's regulated and safe, at what cost?

Answer them and you get a short summary back — what you're investing in, roughly how it's split, and what to actually do next.

⚠️ ps — for education only

This isn't financial advice, and it won't pick stocks for you. It's a framework to help you think clearly. Please do your own research — and talk to a licensed advisor — before putting real money at risk.
